The Front Steps

Investigators might explore here, near to where they met Sam the courier, in order to examine three statues that featured in the currently mundane stereographs (St. George, and Joan of Arc with a separate lion statue).

Players might be familiar with an iconic bronze statue of Sir Redmond Barry that also stands on the forecourt. Try to avoid it as it just gets confused with the marble bust of Barry.

Joan of Arc sits astride a bronze warhorse, armoured from head to heel, her banner raised high as if calling soldiers to follow. The horse walks forward, calm and certain. Nearer the steps a stone lion lies on its plinth, paws outstretched, watching the trams on Swanston Street with weary patience.

St. George rides a rearing bronze horse high on a granite pedestal, the dragon twisting beneath its hooves. The saint leans into the killing blow, and the dragon’s jaws gape up at him, green with age. The pigeons have no respect for either of them.

The Joan card changes. Once Sam has gone, the Joan of Arc card changes before investigators’ eyes the next time they look (new below). The spirit is trying to draw attention to a battle. St. George is already slaying his dragon, so it changed the other card.
