In the 1920s, the State Library (then the Public Library of Victoria), the National Museum and the National Gallery of Victoria occupied the same complex. The above image is an annotated version of Handout C Floorplans. If your group likes maps, provide it when investigators first step inside; map-free games can skip it.

The entry steps, where investigators meet the courier, are on the left (west) side of the floorplan, facing Swanston Street.

The octagonal “Public Library” in the floorplans is the dome seen in Handout A Melbourne.

Guide for Visitors

Swanston Street, Melbourne · Admission Free

  1. 1Museum wing/foyer: To Library (east), to Verdon Gallery (south).
  2. 2Public Library: the octagonal reading room.
  3. 3Verdon Gallery: East-west rows of viewing cabinets through to Stawell Gallery (north) or McArthur Gallery (east).
  4. 4Stawell Gallery: Statues. To Latrobe Gallery (north, then east) or McCoy Hall (east).
  5. 5Latrobe Gallery: Assorted.
  6. 6McArthur Gallery: Featuring new portraits.
  7. 7McCoy Hall: the large central Museum. Latrobe Street entrance currently closed. Staff storage access.
