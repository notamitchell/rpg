Eliza looks down on everyone from the central podium (see below), in her official role as “Chief Shusher”. Behind the podium hangs a board of keys on hooks, each labelled. While she is busy, a key can be lifted (Sleight of Hand).

New to Melbourne, she can’t remember where the Barry bust is: “it was overlooking me from the 5th floor, but they temporarily moved it to one of the galleries”.

When investigators are done with research, Eliza shows them one last stereograph, of her own podium: The Chief Shusher (p. 11).

Eliza: the (Optional) failsafe

Ideally, investigators find their own way to the staff door in McCoy Hall and pick its lock.

Meanwhile, Eliza follows up their questions. One name keeps turning up: Charlie, the archivist, who borrowed a Cyclorama booklet, works on a canvas in basement storage, and never returned several Eureka stereographs.

If the investigation falters, Eliza finds the investigators and shares this.
