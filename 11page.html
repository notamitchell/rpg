The Chief Shusher’s Card

Eliza’s card. Eliza points investigators to a stereograph of her podium, then hurries off to help an important patron. She sees none of what follows.

Each time an investigator looks, the photograph has moved on, as if the camera were flying around the library: past the balconies, up into the dome, along its skylights. (Spot: The label down the side never changes). It lingers a moment on the skylights (Insight: it changed its mind). At last it settles, and stays, on one of the spiral staircases that go to the upper levels. But the right print is upside down.
