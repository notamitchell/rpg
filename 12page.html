Verdon Gallery

Verdon Gallery display cabinet, stereograph by B. C. Boake.

The Verdon Gallery is a long hall filled with glass cabinets, forming something of a minor maze. Some cabinets have mirror backings to aid patrons in viewing the rear angles of assorted artefacts and doodahs.
