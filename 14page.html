Across from Circe is a white marble bust of another woman, in stark contrast to the first. The plaque on the pedestal simply reads “Melba” (see above).

Melba’s touring will one day give Australians the saying “more farewells than Nellie Melba”.

Thematically, Melba’s endless farewells echo the battle in the basement: every time someone crosses in, the dead give one more performance, and every last performance is followed by one more. You might include two passing NPCs who remark on “not being able to imagine singing the same song, night after night, it would send me mad. She should just lay down and rest. Enjoy her retirement.”

Dame Nellie Melba

The Farewell Edition

A household name and an opera singer of great renown, she took her stage name from her home city of Melbourne.

Two years ago Dame Nellie began her grand farewell to the opera. No end is yet in sight: every last performance is followed by one more.

This Farewell Edition follows her from a girlhood in Richmond to Covent Garden, Paris and New York, and home again. With eight photographic plates and a full list of her farewells, correct at the time of press.
