Roe’s Stereograph

Roe found a stereograph on the ground by the Staff Only door (see right).

Spot: The label and index say Dinosaur skeletons.

Spot/Insight: It’s the ceiling of the museum, but upside down!

This is a clue to the basement, and a hint to the way out of the painting (p. 26).

What Mr Roe wants

Commercial Liaison Officer to the CSIR’s new occult branch, Mr Roe is the “practical man” in the flesh and in middle management.

“Applied-science and applied-occult is the way of the future. Theory is a waste of money!”

Mr Roe has paperwork for the investigators: provide Handout F C.S.I.R. Form 7a.

After the Meeting

With excellent lock-picking skills, Roe surreptitiously follows the investigators down the staff staircase a few minutes behind them, either on their trail or on Eliza’s directions.

He might find any or all of the clues investigators left behind and bring them with him.

He spots the bloody handprint on the canvas edge and wonders if it is fresh blood. Touching it causes him to enter the canvas.

Roe might do the same in front of investigators who have been reluctant to touch the canvas, taking them all in with him.

Alternatively, he might see investigators within the artwork and touch the painting, thinking it must be freshly painted.

What Mr Roe knows

  • Cycloramas: nothing at all.
  • The Eureka Rebellion: a passing knowledge, if investigators happen to raise the topic.

    “Typical ‘no taxation without representation’ argument. Town built a stockade to protest. Authorities overreacted and there was something of a massacre.”

    “Ended up hurting the government’s cause more than it helped stop discontent. I seem to recall no jury would convict any of the rebels.”

The Staff Staircase

At the back of the hall, a heavy locked door.

Fine Manipulation: The lock gives. Beyond the door, stone stairs lead down to the basement stores. No luck? Keys hang behind Librarian Eliza’s podium (p. 8) and on Security Guard River’s belt (p. 12). If investigators have angered the Security Guard by messing with the lights or alarm, River will be on patrol and alert near Eliza’s podium.
