The stairs end in a storeroom, shelved to the ceiling with crates. A single electric bulb flickers overhead. Under it, a very large canvas lies flat on a table, a battle in oils. Beside the table, half-propped against its leg, is a body with a dagger in the stomach. The blood beneath it is still wet.

This is Charlie, the archivist.

Eureka Stockade Canvas

Provide Handout G Eureka Painting.

On the table, beside the canvas, is a second dagger and a pouch with a belt loop. It holds twenty-seven gold teeth, roots and all.

Appraise dagger: the dagger is the twin of the one in Charlie.

Appraise teeth: three sets of nine, each set identical.

If players have not already found the booklet, Handout E Eureka Cyclorama is amongst the artwork’s paperwork. Its section The Picture describes the painting.
