Getting Out

The way out is up. The painters’ carriages reach the beams, and the beams reach the skylights. Spot: through the grimy glass, the dim basement storeroom, seen from the canvas itself. A way out of this strange place!

Before anyone climbs

Who has Charlie’s pouch, or the dagger from the table? Who took the dagger or the three teeth from Charlie’s body? Who picked up a sword or other weapon inside that does not belong to them? Players often miss these!

The Climb

1The carriage ladder. No roll: three rounds to the top (two levels per round), one climber per ladder. Pulling the ladder up behind you takes an action and costs pursuers two rounds.

2The horseman. Once the first climber is halfway up, the horseman whose dagger killed Charlie hitches his stuffed horse to the carriage and drags it round the rail in an effort to make investigators fall. He wants the visits to stop and assumes every climber will be back just like Charlie, unless something convinces him otherwise (Gamemaster’s call). On a moving carriage, each round of climbing needs Climb (fail: no progress; fumble: fall, 1D6 per 10 feet / 3 m).

3The beams. From a moving carriage, they whizz past a beam every round: Jump (miss: back on the deck, 1D3; fumble: over the side, a fall as in step 2). Bring down the horse, unseat the rider or cut the traces, and the carriage slows after 1 round. Getting to the beams needs no roll from a stationary carriage. Bringing up a ladder might help injured investigators make the final exit.

4The skylight. Along the beam and up through the glass. The climber comes out of the canvas in the basement storeroom (all paint left behind), beside the table, as Charlie did.

The Party Outside

Investigators outside the canvas, because they were elsewhere when it was touched or climbed out first, can potentially help by finding every stolen item, the pouch, both daggers and Charlie’s pocket, and throwing each one at the canvas.

They can see events unfold in the canvas. Perhaps Mr Roe, a familiar face on a painted horse. They can also hear what’s going on inside the Cyclorama.

Smart parties might try a strategic reset: entering the canvas snaps moving dummies back to their places, preventing the next fatal blow! But anyone in the storeroom when the canvas is touched goes back in too!
