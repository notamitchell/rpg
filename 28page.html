Whatever the Outcome

If the busker played earlier, he returns, cap in hand, with one more song (optional). Provide players Handout H The Busker’s Ballad (Reprise) and have a player perform it.

Handing in the paperwork. Even if only one investigator escaped, someone has to fill it in. The CSIR expects Form 7a (Handout F C.S.I.R. Form 7a), completed in triplicate. What the investigators write decides the canvas’s fate. A “No” to “Can it be patented, bottled or licensed?” closes the file. A “Yes” means the CSIR sends another practical man (see Investigation Failure). The balance of the fee arrives either way; after a “Yes” it is larger, with a share of the licence pencilled in.

Mr Roe’s absence must be reported separately:

Investigation Success

The investigators escape the living canvas and make sure no one ever enters it again: a “No” on Form 7a with Secure storage ticked, a locked crate, the canvas rolled face-in. The canvas sleeps.

If every stolen item went back, so do the dead of the Stockade, and the hauntings fade. The Russell Street card is just a street. The Eureka memorial just a stereograph. Sir Redmond Barry looks straight ahead, and nowhere else. The Lalor portrait restored.

Anything kept. If anyone pocketed so much as a tooth or a sword, it is a link from the dead to the living. One day it might be a conduit for something else far more sinister.

Investigation Failure

Investigators who die inside the living painting join it, as does Mr Cy Roe if no one returns what was stolen. Worked like puppets, they re-enact the battle every time someone crosses in.

Investigators who escape but leave the canvas where anyone can reach it, or who tell the CSIR it can be licensed, fail more slowly. The canvas becomes a commercial proposition, and every paying visitor wrenches the dead back to fight and fall.

The hauntings spread to every stereograph, painting and photograph of Eureka in Melbourne. The dead are still trying to get a message through.

Years later, a new stereograph turns up on the library’s returns trolley: the Eureka Cyclorama. In the crowd might be faces the investigators’ friends would recognise.
