Appendix Make your own Stereoscope

Sam’s parcel holds three stereographs and a stereoscope. Your players can have them all to play with! Build a viewer from reading glasses and cardboard, and hand it round when the photograph of the bust turns up. Print this page at 100% and cut out the two cards below, the small stereographs from the research boxes on pp. 9–10; those on pp. 5, 12 and 16 print at the same 150 mm width. You can even take your own stereographs: photograph a still scene, step 6.5 cm to the right, photograph it again, then crop the two to the same size and set them side by side.

D.I.Y. Virtual RealityThe Smithsonian’s Lemelson Center for the Study of Invention and Innovation has free instructions for making your own viewer.
SLV LAB stereoscope viewerState Library Victoria’s lab hosts a viewer that simulates the 3D effect on screen, and turns its stereographs into red–blue pictures for 3D glasses.
