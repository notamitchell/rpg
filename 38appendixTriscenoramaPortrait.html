Appendix Triscenorama Portrait

Peter Lalor, 1827–1889
From the left

Administering the oath under the Southern Cross, Eureka Stockade, 1854

At Eureka, 30 November 1854, hundreds knelt bareheaded beneath the Southern Cross and swore to defend their rights and liberties.

From the front

After the fight: wounded, hunted, a price of £200 on his head

Shot at dawn on 3 December, he lay an hour beneath a pile of slabs as police passed within feet; next day the arm came off.

From the right

The Hon. Peter Lalor, Speaker of the Legislative Assembly, 1880–1887

His comrades, tried for high treason before Judge Barry, were acquitted; the fugitive went to Parliament for Ballarat.

A bold modern take on three existing portraits, united in a single Triscenorama.

Composite of three portraits of Peter Lalor, by F. A. Sleap, Ludwig Becker and Annie C. H. Thunder.
