We knew that science was a weapon, and not just a hobby, Best held by men of industry, the business-interest lobby!

When pressed to front the tab, he dug his heels in: not a quid! The P.M. shot the bleating down, the wily Billy the Kid!

Now Stanley Bruce is leader, and he’s made the nation’s news, And that’s the resignation of our P.M. Billy Hughes.

In twenty-six, young Brucey went and gave the Act his seal, To turn the lofty theory into something made of steel:

A COUNCIL For SCIENTIFIC And INDUSTRIAL RESEARCH!

Oh P.M. Brucey, Brucey, you’ve put science on a leash, No iv’ry towers any more, no scholarly caprice.

The lab is now a factory, the test tube is a tool. The businessman is teaching the professor how to school.
