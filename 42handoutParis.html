About this booklet

This appears to be a booklet for some kind of painting exhibition, along with historical context on the topic of the artwork, and “how the Cyclorama was made”. The large doughnut-shaped diagram below immediately stands out, as do some sketches of historical figures. Towards the end of the booklet is a clue as to the nature of a Cyclorama:

“A large canvas of about 50ft. x 400ft. is carefully stretched in a circular building just as you see it on exhibition, except there is a tramway all around close to the canvas, upon which are moveable staging or carriages of different heights to enable the artists to readily reach any section.”

The Cyclorama of the Siege of Paris, compiled by B. A. Reeve, 1891.
