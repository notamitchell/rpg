Handout H The Busker’s Ballad (Reprise)

Sir Redmond let the jury judge, and let the rebels walk... Right down to where the Library stood, hands clean, and came to gawk.

Oh Sir Redmond Barry, Barry, what made you set them free? They hang immortalised in oils inside your Library.

Sir Redmond built a Uni just for learning’s own sweet sake; Now pollies want “job-ready” crowds: no Homer, Keats or Blake.

They said they’d democratise research, and give the folk a say; The folk turned out to be a board, the bureaucratic way!

The scientist, the polly and the man who owns the mine Still argue who should hold the reins, and who should toe the line.

They say it’s for the people, and the ivory tower’s dead; They asked your eye-teeth for the cause... then plucked them out instead!

The practical man went hunting for a licence he could sell. He found the painted diggers, and they didn’t take it well.

They’d put his cart before the horse; no scratch, it wouldn’t go, Till a horse was paid for dearly, and they christened him Cy Roe.

It should have been old Billy who was turned into a beast! At least we’d get some cash from him: a Billy-goat well fleeced!

For Barry was a stereograph, two pictures side by side: The people’s right to knowledge, and the scholar’s right to guide.

Hold theory to your left eye, and your practice to the right: In stereo they are as one; remove an eye, they fight:

C! See all the money flow! S! Essential that we grow! I! I really want to know, R! Are your blinkers on, Cy Roe? O! Oh no, no Oh!  No, no!!

(The “O” would not be added on till 1949. So let’s all hear it for the old CSIR design!)
